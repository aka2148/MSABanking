| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|

| Application configuration → financial policy | Missing or malformed policy values could silently change money/date behavior. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-01 | Tampering | `FdLifecycleStateMachine` | mitigate | Accept only the four named commands and four exact transitions; reject every other pair with `InvalidFdTransitionException`. |
| T-01-02 | Tampering | `FdCalculationPolicy` / `FdTimePolicy` | mitigate | Validate all policy fields, require a version, and derive dates only from injected `Clock` and configured `ZoneId`. |
| T-01-03 | Repudiation | lifecycle result | accept | Durable actor/event evidence is owned by the later mutation services; this plan keeps the pure policy free of actor and persistence concerns. |
</threat_model>
