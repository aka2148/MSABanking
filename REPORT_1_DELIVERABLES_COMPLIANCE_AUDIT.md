### Evidence and status rules

Static code presence alone never earns `PASS`. A status describes **manual compliance**, not whether a service returned HTTP 200. ### Accounting model and operational truth

The FD service stores customer-facing `savings_accounts.sa_balance`, `savings_transactions`, `fd_transactions`, and selected `fd_journals`/`fd_journal_lines`. Funding, maturity interest, and normal settlement have two-line journal code. Demo opening, teller deposit/withdraw, and loan disbursement directly change the savings balance/history without a journal; transfer and actual forex exchange have no active posting path. No complete chart-of-accounts constraint, account-level reconciliation job, reversal/correction workflow, or proof that all savings balances derive from a journal was found. The live aggregate proves **per-journal balance** for 11 journals and **failed liability clearance** for two premature accounts; it cannot prove overall customer balances reconcile or identify duplicate business postings. Financial entries must be evaluated **per currency**. The current calculator/Angular precision conflicts with configured JPY zero decimals, and KWD three decimals needs explicit coverage ([financial evidence](evidence/financial_integrity.md)). A bank-like reconciliation and separation of paying/accounting functions are sound control goals, consistent with [Basel Core Principle 26](https://www.bis.org/committees/bcbs/basel-framework/standard/bcp?allChapters=true); they are design references for a lab, not a claim that this project is a regulated bank.
